ReviewQR Reviews from people who were actually there

One QR code. One review per customer.

Put the code on the counter, the receipt or the table. Customers scan it, see your latest reviews, verify their email once and leave one review.

How it works

  1. A customer scans your code and lands on your profile.
  2. They verify their email with a 6-digit code. Returning customers skip this.
  3. They pick a star rating, tap a few topics and add an optional note.
  4. We suggest a handful of drafts from exactly those details. They pick one and edit it.
  5. They post it. One review per person, per business.

Drafts are built only from what the customer tells us. Nothing is invented, and every draft is editable before it is posted.
